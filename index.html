When to go in person instead

Online care is not right for emergencies. Call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital if you have:

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Trouble breathing
  • Heavy bleeding
  • Sudden weakness, confusion or fainting
  • Thoughts of harming yourself

Questions people ask

How do I join my consultation?

We send a joining link by SMS and email once you book, and again shortly before your slot. Open it on your phone or computer. You do not need to install anything.

Will I get a prescription?

If the doctor decides medicine is needed and it is safe to prescribe online, you receive a written prescription after the call. Some conditions need an in-person examination or tests first, and the doctor will tell you so.

Is my consultation private?

Yes. Only you and your doctor take part in the call, and your notes are visible only to you and the doctors you choose to share them with.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

You can reschedule or cancel from the link in your confirmation message, up to 30 minutes before your slot.

What if the doctor wants me to get tests?

The doctor will explain which tests you need and why. You can take the request to any lab, then book a follow-up call to go through the results.
